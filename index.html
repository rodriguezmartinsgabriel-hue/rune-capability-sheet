  el().key("root").w(W).h(H).fill(t.bg)
    .child(el().key("grain").w(W).h(H)

Full source: grain_compose.rs

Three.js grain motor
141k particles rendered to MP4. Rune decodes it synchronously in headless runs and composites native UI on top. Proven on this sheet.
Blender 4.5
Render a 3D scene to PNG frames or movie, then feed Rune as ImageCache or Player. Ideal for offline cinematic passes.
Godot 4.7.2
Real-time 3D frames via NativeSurface::push_shared on Windows or push_dmabuf on Linux.
Custom wgpu
Compositor hands device, queue, encoder and target. Write your own depth buffer and particle pipeline; force GpuBackend::Wgpu on macOS.
Web
Two canvases in the DOM: Three.js underneath, Rune WebGPU on top with App::canvas_id.
